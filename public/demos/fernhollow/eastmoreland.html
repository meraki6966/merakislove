What should Eastmoreland pet owners know?

Crystal Springs Rhododendron Garden sits between Reed College and the Eastmoreland Golf Course, and leashed dogs are welcome there. It is one of the best walks in the city in April and May.

Rhododendrons and azaleas are toxic to dogs and cats if the leaves or flowers are chewed. Most pets leave them alone. Puppies and bored young dogs sometimes do not, and a dog that has chewed the leaves and is drooling or vomiting belongs on the red list.

Old trees also mean mushrooms once the fall rain starts. Clear the yard after the first wet week, and bring a photo if your dog eats one.

A short drive west across McLoughlin into Sellwood. There is parking behind the clinic.

Can I bring my dog to Crystal Springs Rhododendron Garden?

Yes, on a leash. Keep puppies from chewing the plants, since rhododendron leaves are toxic to dogs.

My dog ate a mushroom in the yard. What do I do?

Take a photo of the mushroom and go to the emergency hospital. DoveLewis is open 24 hours at (503) 228-7281.
