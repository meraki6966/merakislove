What happens at a sick visit?

  1. You call

    Tell us what you are seeing, when it started, and whether your pet is eating and drinking. We book the first open slot or tell you to go straight to the emergency hospital.

  2. You arrive

    Cats go to the quiet room. Dogs come in through the side door if the lobby is busy.

  3. We examine

    A veterinarian does a full exam, including the parts that seem unrelated. The cause is often somewhere you were not looking.

  4. You get an estimate

    Any test or treatment is written down with its price. You approve it before we start.

  5. You go home with a plan

    Medication, what to watch for, and when to come back. A technician calls the next business day to check in.

When is it more than a sick visit?

Some things cannot wait for a clinic slot. Trouble breathing, collapse, a swollen belly with retching, a cat that cannot urinate, and any poisoning are emergencies. Go to DoveLewis Veterinary Emergency and Specialty Hospital at 1945 NW Pettygrove Street, open 24 hours, or call (503) 228-7281.

If you call us during the day with one of those, we will say so and help you get there. The emergency page lists the signs for dogs and for cats.

For everything on the yellow list, a same day visit is the right speed. Waiting two or three days is how a simple problem becomes an expensive one.

What if I call after 3 PM?

We book the first visit the next morning and tell you what to watch for overnight. If it gets worse, call DoveLewis at (503) 228-7281.

Can I walk in?

Please call first. A two minute call lets us tell you whether to come to us or go straight to the emergency hospital, and it means a room is ready.
