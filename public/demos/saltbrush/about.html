Our story

Why did Saltbrush start?

Nico Velarde spent six summers on another company's routes, servicing more pools a day than anyone could do well. Customers rarely knew his name, and he rarely had time to brush. He left to build a company around two rules: fewer pools per route, and a report after every visit.

Those two rules still run the business. A technician has about half an hour at each pool. The report goes out before the truck leaves your street. Everything else, from the flat monthly price to the way gate codes are stored, follows from wanting customers to be able to check our work.

We stay inside a small part of the Valley on purpose. Tight routes through Arcadia, Ahwatukee and Tempe mean less time on the freeway and more time with a brush in the water.

Licensing

What is Saltbrush licensed to do?

The scope of Arizona's R-6 classification, in plain words.

  1. Covered by our R-6 license

    Service and minor repair of residential pools and accessories: pumps, filters, salt systems, automation, valves and equipment plumbing.

  2. Outside it

    Connections to a drinking water line, gas lines, gas chlorine systems, electrical work beyond the first disconnect, and full replacement of plaster, pebble or decks.

  3. What we do then

    We tell you which licensed trade to call, what to ask for, and we coordinate our part of the job around theirs.

Scope summarized from the Arizona Registrar of Contractors residential classification R-6, Swimming Pool Service and Repair. See equipment repair for what that looks like on a job.

Questions owners ask

About Saltbrush, answered.

A few things people ask before they hire us.

How long has Saltbrush been in business?

Since 2014. We started with forty pools in central Phoenix and grew one route at a time.

How many pools does each technician service?

We cap routes so a technician has about half an hour at each pool. A route that is too long is how brushing gets skipped.

Are your technicians employees?

Yes. Every technician is an employee who is trained, background checked and insured. We do not hand routes to subcontractors.

What does the name mean?

Saltbush is a tough desert shrub that grows all over the Valley, and salt and a brush are two things a pool technician uses every day. The name is a nod to both.
