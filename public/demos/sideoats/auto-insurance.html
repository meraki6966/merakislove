What does 30/60/25 mean?

It is the least auto liability coverage Texas lets you drive with. Each number is in thousands of dollars.

  • 30

    $30,000 for each injured person

    What your policy pays toward one person's injuries when a wreck is your fault.

  • 60

    $60,000 for each accident

    The most it pays for everyone's injuries in one wreck, however many people are hurt.

  • 25

    $25,000 for property damage

    What it pays to fix the other driver's vehicle. Many new trucks cost more than this.

Is the state minimum enough?

The minimum keeps you legal. It was not built to cover a serious wreck. $25,000 for property damage is less than the price of many new vehicles, and $30,000 does not go far in an emergency room.

When a judgment is larger than your limits, the difference can come from your savings and your wages. We price the next two steps alongside the minimum, so you can see what the extra coverage costs before you choose.

Auto insurance, answered

What is the minimum auto insurance in Texas?

Liability coverage of at least $30,000 for each injured person, up to $60,000 per accident, and $25,000 for property damage. It is written as 30/60/25.

Does auto insurance cover hail damage?

Only if you carry comprehensive coverage. Your comprehensive deductible comes out first. See what to do after a hail storm.

What is PIP?

Personal injury protection pays medical bills and some lost income for you and your passengers, whoever caused the wreck. Every Texas auto policy includes it unless you reject it in writing.

Do I need uninsured motorist coverage?

Your insurer has to offer it, and you only go without it if you turn it down in writing. It is what pays when the driver who hit you has no insurance.
