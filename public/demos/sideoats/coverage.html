Choosing coverage, answered

Which policies does a homeowner in Fort Worth usually carry?

Home and auto, and flood if the house is near water or in a mapped flood zone. An umbrella is worth a look once there is equity or savings to protect.

Can you insure my home and car with different companies?

Yes. An independent agency can place each policy where it fits. Bundling them with one carrier often brings a discount, so we price it both ways.

Do you give quotes online?

We give them by phone or in person, after we have read your current policy. A quote that ignores your deductible and limits is a guess.

What does it cost to use an agent?

Nothing extra. The insurance company pays the agency a commission that is already part of the premium.
