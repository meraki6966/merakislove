Why we exist

We believe the best days happen when people step out of their routines and into something real. No agenda. No performance. Just hands in clay, food cooked from what grows here, and conversations that actually go somewhere.

Every experience here is designed around one question: what would this person remember in ten years? That question shapes everything we build.
