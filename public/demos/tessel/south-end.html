About this office

The South End office.

South End grew up around the old rail line, and so did we. Most of our patients here walk or ride the Blue Line in, often on the way to work, which is why we open at 7:30 and keep the first hour for checkups.

The office has five treatment rooms, a quiet consult room for treatment plans, and Dr. Sethuraman in the chair four days a week.

Questions people ask

The South End office, answered.

Parking, hours and getting here.

Is there parking at the South End office?

Yes. The garage behind the building has spaces, and the front desk validates two hours.

Can I take the light rail?

Yes. Ride the LYNX Blue Line to East/West Blvd station and walk north on the Rail Trail. It's about a two minute walk.

Do you have early appointments?

We open at 7:30 AM Monday through Friday, so you can be seen before work.
