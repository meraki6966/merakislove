Who does the work and how we charge.

Do you work with clients outside Philadelphia?

Yes. We prepare returns for clients across Pennsylvania and for people who moved away and still have Pennsylvania or Philadelphia filings. City taxes are our specialty, and most of our clients live or work in Philadelphia or its suburbs.

Who will prepare my return?

A CPA or an enrolled agent on our staff, and a second one reviews it. We do not send work to outside preparers or overseas.

What is the difference between a CPA and an enrolled agent?

A CPA is licensed by the state after an exam and supervised experience, and can perform audits as well as tax work. An enrolled agent is licensed by the IRS and specializes in tax. Both can represent you before the IRS.

Is the first call free?

Yes. It takes about twenty minutes, and you leave it knowing which filings you need and what they will cost.
