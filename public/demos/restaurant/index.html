Our story

Rosa Delgado

Rosa Delgado spent fifteen years cooking in other people’s kitchens before she opened one of her own. In 2016, she took over the corner lot at 4th and Maple, the kind of spot every neighborhood has, and turned it into a place where regulars know the servers by name and the specials board actually changes.

Nothing here is trying to impress you. It’s trying to feed you well.

Rosa Delgado · Chef & Owner
