The craft

Why is it called The Darkroom?

Every photograph in the suite was printed by hand in a darkroom, from film. They show the Gulf in every kind of weather, and the pier in most of them is gone now.

The carriage house once held a buggy and a hayloft. The loft floor came out to make the vaulted ceiling, and the big doors became the French doors to the porch.

The Darkroom, answered

Can we bring children?

Yes. The carriage house is the one place at the inn where children under 12 can stay. A rollaway bed is $25 a night, a sample rate for this demo.

How far is the carriage house from the main house?

About thirty steps across the garden. Breakfast is served on the gallery of the main house, or we bring a tray to your porch.

Is the porch private?

Yes. It faces the garden and belongs to the suite alone.
