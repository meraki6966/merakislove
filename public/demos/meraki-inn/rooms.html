Choosing

How do I choose a room?

Tell Celeste what the trip is for and she will know. Until then, this is how most guests choose.

  • For the morning light

    The Loom, on the southeast corner. See The Loom.

  • For a long bath

    The Kiln, with a clawfoot tub by the window. See The Kiln.

  • For no stairs

    The Press, step free from the gated lot. See The Press.

  • For a rainy day

    The Bindery, with a wall of books. See The Bindery.

  • For a family, or a porch of your own

    The Darkroom, in the carriage house. See The Darkroom.

The rooms, answered

Does every room have a private bath?

Yes. Four rooms have a walk in shower, and The Kiln has a clawfoot tub with a hand shower.

Is breakfast included in every rate?

Yes. Coffee is out at 6:30 and breakfast is cooked to order from 8 to 9:30, for every guest.

Which room has no stairs?

The Press. It is on the first floor and is step free from the gated lot, by the side porch ramp.

Do you have a room for three?

The Darkroom. It is a suite in the carriage house, and a rollaway bed is $25 a night, a sample rate for this demo.
