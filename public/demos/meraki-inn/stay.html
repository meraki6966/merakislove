Tax

What does tax add to a room in Galveston?

Hotel occupancy tax adds 15 percent. The State of Texas charges 6 percent of the cost of a room and the City of Galveston charges 9 percent.

Two nights in The Loom at $249 come to $498. Tax adds $74.70, for a total of $572.70.

Nothing else is added. There is no resort fee and no charge for parking, bikes, wifi or breakfast.

The house rules

What are the policies?

These are samples for this demo, written the way a live inn would write them.

  • Check in and check out

    Check in from 3 PM to 8 PM. Check out by 11 AM. Call if you will be late and we will leave the porch light on.

  • Minimum stay

    Two nights on Friday and Saturday. Three nights for Dickens on The Strand and both Mardi Gras weekends.

  • Deposit and payment

    One night is charged when we confirm your dates, by card, over the phone. The rest is charged at check in.

  • Cancelling

    Cancel seven days ahead for a full refund. For festival weekends it is fourteen days.

  • Storms

    A hurricane warning for Galveston during your dates means a full refund. See hurricane season.

  • Children

    Children 12 and older are welcome in the house. Children of any age are welcome in The Darkroom.

  • Pets and smoking

    No pets, and no smoking anywhere on the property.

  • Parking

    Free for guests, in the gated lot behind the house.

Rates and policies, answered

What time is check in at the inn?

From 3 PM to 8 PM. Check out is by 11 AM.

Is there a minimum stay?

Two nights on Friday and Saturday, and three nights for Dickens on The Strand and both Mardi Gras weekends.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancel seven days ahead for a full refund, or fourteen days ahead for festival weekends. A hurricane warning for Galveston during your dates always means a full refund.

How much is the tax on a room in Galveston?

15 percent. The state hotel occupancy tax is 6 percent and the City of Galveston adds 9 percent.

Do you take a deposit?

Yes. One night, taken by card over the phone when we confirm your dates.
