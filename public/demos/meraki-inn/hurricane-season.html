The forecast

How do we keep you informed?

The policy is tied to the official watch and warning, so no one has to guess.

  • Season dates

    The Atlantic hurricane season runs from June 1 to November 30.

  • Advisories every six hours

    At 4 AM, 10 AM, 4 PM and 10 PM Central daylight time. When a coastal watch or warning is in effect, shorter updates come every three hours in between.

  • A message from us

    If a watch is posted for Galveston, we email every guest booked that week the same day.

  • Your call, with time to make it

    A watch gives about two days of notice. You never have to decide before the forecast does.

The island

Is Galveston a good idea in hurricane season?

Most summer and fall days on the island are ordinary beach days. The water is warm, the rates are lower after Labor Day, and the porch is at its best in October.

The Seawall and the grade raising were built for the other days. A forecast gives days of notice, and our policy is written so that a storm never costs you the room.

The policy covers your stay with us. It does not cover a flight, a cruise or a rental car, so travel insurance can still be worth having.

Storm season, answered

When is hurricane season in Galveston?

June 1 to November 30.

Do I get my money back if a hurricane is forecast?

If a hurricane warning is issued for Galveston during your dates, we refund the stay in full. If a hurricane watch is posted, you can move your dates at no charge.

What about a tropical storm?

A tropical storm warning for the island lets you move your dates at no charge.

Do I still need travel insurance?

Our policy covers your room. It does not cover a flight, a cruise or a rental car, so that is a decision for you and your insurer.
