Frequently asked

Service area questions.

Coverage, arrival windows and permits across the metro.

Which areas do you serve?

Columbus, Dublin, Westerville and Grove City, plus Hilliard, Upper Arlington, Worthington, Gahanna and Powell. If you're close to any of those, call and ask.

Do you charge extra for travel?

No. The diagnostic visit price is the same anywhere in our service area.

Who handles permits in each city?

We do. Each city runs its own permit office and inspection schedule, and we file and schedule with whichever one covers your address.
