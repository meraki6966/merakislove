Working in Columbus

Old houses, new demands.

Columbus has some of the oldest housing in Ohio. German Village and Victorian Village still have homes from the 1800s, and much of Clintonville went up between the 1910s and the 1950s.

That means galvanized pipe, cast iron drains, small electrical panels and boilers that have been patched for decades. We work on those houses every day, and we know which upgrades make sense in a house that old and which ones don't.

Where we work in Columbus.

A sample of the Columbus neighborhoods we're in most weeks.

01

German Village

Brick cottages from the 1800s on narrow lots and brick streets. Tight basements, original drain lines and lots of careful work in finished spaces.

02

Clintonville

Homes from the 1910s to the 1950s with mature trees and older sewer laterals. Root intrusion and galvanized supply lines are common calls.

03

Short North

Rehabbed historic buildings next to new condos. Mixed wiring generations, tankless water heaters and condo association rules.

04

Victorian Village

Large Victorian homes, many converted to doubles. Boilers, knob and tube wiring and multiple meters.

Frequently asked

Columbus questions, answered.

Written around what people in Columbus search for before they call.

Do you work on historic homes in German Village?

Yes, often. We plan around finished plaster, tight basements and exterior rules, and we'll talk through options that keep the character of the house.

Can you replace galvanized pipe without opening every wall?

Usually most of it. We run new lines through basements, closets and chases, and plan any wall openings with you before we start.

How fast can you get to a Columbus address?

Columbus calls usually get a same day, two hour window because our dispatch is here.
