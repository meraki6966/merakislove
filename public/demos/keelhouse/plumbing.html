When to call

Signs it's time for a visit.

If one of these sounds familiar, a diagnostic visit usually finds the cause in under an hour.

  • Rusty or brown water when a tap first opens
  • Water pressure that dropped over the years
  • A water heater older than ten years, or one that rumbles
  • Drains that gurgle or back up into another fixture
  • A sump pump that runs constantly, or not at all
  • A water bill that jumped without a change in use

Frequently asked

Plumbing questions.

Written for the searches people run when something stops working.

How much does a water heater replacement cost in Columbus?

Most tank replacements land between $1,650 and $2,400 depending on size, fuel and venting. Tankless installs start around $3,900. You get the exact price in writing first.

Should I switch to a tankless water heater?

It makes sense for bigger households and homes with room for the venting. With Central Ohio's hard water, plan on a yearly descale. The plumber will walk you through both options on site.

How do I know if I have galvanized pipes?

Scratch an exposed pipe in the basement with a key. Galvanized steel is dull gray and a magnet sticks to it. Copper shows a bright penny color. If you're unsure, the plumber can check during any visit.

Do you handle sewer line problems?

Yes. We start with a camera inspection so you can see the problem, then quote a repair or a lining option before anything is dug up.
