When to call

Signs it's time for a visit.

If one of these sounds familiar, a diagnostic visit usually finds the cause in under an hour.

  • Furnace runs but the house won't warm up
  • Breaker trips when the AC kicks on
  • Water pooling around the furnace or air handler
  • Energy bill jumped without a change in use
  • One room always hotter or colder than the rest
  • System is 15 or more years old

Frequently asked

Heating and Cooling questions.

Written for the searches people run when something stops working.

How much does a furnace repair cost in Columbus?

The diagnostic visit is $120 and comes off the bill if you go ahead. Common repairs like an ignitor or flame sensor run $240 to $450. You get the exact price before any work starts.

Should I repair or replace my furnace?

If it's under 12 years old and the repair is less than a third of the cost of a new system, repair usually wins. The tech walks you through both numbers on site.

Do heat pumps work in an Ohio winter?

Today's cold climate heat pumps heat well into the single digits. Many Columbus homes pair one with the existing gas furnace as a backup for the coldest days, which is called a dual fuel setup.

How often should I change my furnace filter?

Every one to three months for a standard one inch filter, more often with pets. Thicker media filters can go six months or more.
