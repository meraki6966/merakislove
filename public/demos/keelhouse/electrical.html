When to call

Signs it's time for a visit.

Some of these are urgent. If you smell burning or see scorch marks, turn the circuit off and call the emergency line.

  • Breakers that trip when two things run at once
  • Lights that dim when the AC or a big appliance starts
  • Outlets that feel warm or have scorch marks
  • Two prong outlets throughout an older house
  • Planning an EV charger, heat pump or hot tub
  • A panel from the 1980s or earlier, or a Federal Pacific panel

Frequently asked

Electrical questions.

Written for the searches people run when something stops working.

How much does a panel upgrade cost in Columbus?

Most 100 to 200 amp upgrades run $2,400 to $3,800, including the permit, utility coordination and inspection. You get the exact price in writing first.

Do I need a panel upgrade for an EV charger?

Not always. A load calculation tells us whether your panel has room. Many homes can add a Level 2 charger on the existing panel, sometimes with a smart load management device.

Will I lose power during a panel upgrade?

For part of one day. We coordinate the disconnect and reconnect with the utility and schedule around your plans.

Are two prong outlets dangerous?

They aren't grounded, which matters for computers, TVs and anything with a three prong plug. We can ground them or add GFCI protection to meet code.
